FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about air duct and dryer vent cleaning in White Plains, NY.

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

The EPA recommends having your air ducts cleaned every 3 to 5 years. However, you may need more frequent cleaning if you have pets, allergies, recently completed renovations, visible mold growth or notice excessive dust accumulation in your White Plains home or business.

Why is dryer vent cleaning important?

Clogged dryer vents are one of the leading causes of house fires in the United States. Lint accumulates in the vent over time and can ignite easily. Regular cleaning also shortens drying times, reduces energy consumption and extends the life of your dryer. We recommend annual cleaning for most Westchester homes.

Do you serve all of Westchester County?

Yes, we serve White Plains and all surrounding communities in Westchester County, New York. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for both residential and commercial clients. Contact us at 914-770-7709 to confirm service availability in your specific area.

Are your cleaning methods safe and eco-friendly?

Yes, we use only eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning techniques and products. Our methods are completely safe for children, pets and individuals with allergies or sensitivities. We use advanced equipment to physically remove contaminants rather than relying on harsh chemical agents.

Can air duct cleaning reduce my energy bills?

Yes. When air ducts are clogged with dust and debris, your HVAC system has to work harder to maintain the desired temperature, using more energy in the process. Clean ducts allow air to flow freely, which can noticeably reduce your heating and cooling costs throughout the year in your Westchester home or business.

Our dryer vent cleaning service in White Plains and Westchester County thoroughly removes lint, debris and blockages from the full length of your dryer exhaust system.
